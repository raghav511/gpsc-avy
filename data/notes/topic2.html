Memory hook: picture each remedy as a person with one sentence: Aconite is "sudden fear in cold dry wind", Bryonia is "lie still, worse any motion". If you can say the sentence, you have the remedy.

Homoeopathic Materia Medica is the record of the pathogenetic effects of drugs, found by proving on healthy people, together with toxicological data and clinical confirmations. Hahnemann's Materia Medica Pura (1811 to 1821, six volumes) is the first pure MM; Fragmenta de viribus (1805) recorded 27 drugs.

Sources of the Materia Medica

  1. Drug provings on healthy people: the primary source (Organon §§105 to 145).

  2. Toxicology: accidental and intentional poisonings, which show the gross effects.

  3. Clinical observations: symptoms cured though never proved, marked as clinical.

  4. Animal experiments, for tissue changes only.

Modalities

A modality is a condition that makes a symptom better (amel., >) or worse (agg., <): time, temperature, weather, position, motion, touch, eating, emotions. Modalities turn a common symptom into a characteristic one, and Boenninghausen generalised them to the whole patient.

The common teaching cycle Sulphur → Calcarea carb → Lycopodium (and back to Sulphur) is asked often; the order is not reversed: Calcarea is not given before Sulphur.

Memory hook: C-I-A — Complementary completes, Inimical injures, Antidote annuls. For inimicals chant "Cause Phos-trouble, Apis Rhus-hes in, Merc Sil-ences": Causticum–Phosphorus, Apis–Rhus tox, Mercurius–Silicea.

Families worth knowing

  • Solanaceae: Belladonna, Hyoscyamus, Stramonium (the three delirium remedies), Capsicum, Dulcamara.

  • Loganiaceae: Nux vomica, Ignatia, Gelsemium, Spigelia.

  • Ranunculaceae: Aconite, Pulsatilla, Staphisagria.

  • Rubiaceae: Cinchona, Ipecac, Coffea.

  • Compositae: Arnica, Chamomilla, Eupatorium.

Memory hook: the delirium trio by temperature of mind: Bell burns (hot, violent, red), Hyos hides nothing (jealous, shameless, uncovers), Stram screams for light (terror of the dark). And for motion: Bryonia freezes, Rhus tox keeps walking.

Memory hook: the Natrum family splits by weather: Nat mur hates the sun, Nat carb hates the sun and milk, Nat sulph hates the damp. And the Calc pair by food: Calc carb wants eggs, Calc phos wants bacon.

Definitions

  • Nosodes are prepared from diseased tissue or the products of disease (discharges, cultures, morbid tissue). Hering introduced the idea; Burnett and Swan widened its use.

  • Sarcodes are prepared from healthy animal tissues and secretions: Thyroidinum, Adrenalinum, Pituitrinum, Insulinum, Lac caninum.

  • Imponderabilia are energies with no material substance: X-ray, Magnetis poli, Sol, Luna, Electricitas.

  • A nosode is often used as an intercurrent remedy when the well-chosen remedy stops acting or a miasm blocks progress.

Source questions often hide a trap: Apis, Cantharis and Spongia are animal remedies even when listed with minerals.
