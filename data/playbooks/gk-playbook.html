What the papers show

The GK pattern in five rules

1

Current affairs now dominate

The current-affairs share rose from about 3 in 10 questions (2021) to nearly 8 in 10 (2025). The September 2026 papers were about 95% current affairs.

2

The window is short

About three-quarters of current questions are about events 0 to 3 months before the exam. Papers held in January–February also ask "in 2025…" questions about the previous year.

3

"Which state…first…" is the favourite stem

About 1 in 6 GK questions asks which state, city or district did something first or launched something.

4

Days are asked by date, not theme

81% of important-day questions ask the date ("On which date is X observed?"). Only 15% ask the year's theme.

5

Goa is about 1 in 10

Expect about one Goa question per paper: a 2025–26 scheme, event or award, or static history and geography. Administrative posts (Mamlatdar) add Goa laws and Panchayati Raj.

6

Static GK is the floor

Teacher and Mamlatdar papers (Jan–Feb 2026) carried 4–6 static questions each: polity articles and amendments, superlatives in geography, and firsts in history.

Study window

What to read, and how far back

The Homoeopathic Physician CBRT (Advt. 06/2026) is on 1 November 2026. On the pattern of recent papers, most current-affairs questions will come from August to October 2026, so read those months first, then the rest of 2026.

Last 3 months: read daily

  • States and firsts, sports results, appointments, defence exercises and ships
  • Days falling in those months, with dates
  • Goa news: schemes, events, awards

3 to 12 months back: one pass

  • Big events: Asian Games, CWG, FIFA World Cup, T20 World Cup, BRICS summit, budget
  • Major appointments: CJI, VP, RBI, service chiefs
  • Goa budget and new schemes

Static: keep warm

  • Polity articles, amendments, bodies
  • Geography superlatives and nicknames
  • Goa history, rivers, sanctuaries, temples, firsts

#1 · Static

Goa: history, geography, culture and laws

about 7% of all GK since 2018static

Dec 2021Goa was liberated by the Indian army in an action known as: Operation Vijay

Oct 2022Which river has the largest basin in Goa? Mandovi

Jan 2026The Comunidade system in Goa is best described as: a collective landholding institution

History and freedom struggle

  • Ancient name Gomantak; Kadamba port-capital Gopakapattana (Goa Velha), trading with Sri Lanka, Zanzibar, Gujarat
  • Medieval order: Bhojas, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Shilaharas, Kadambas, Vijayanagara, Bahmani, Adil Shah
  • Afonso de Albuquerque captured Goa in 1510
  • Pinto Revolt (1787): conspiracy of Goan priests against Portuguese rule
  • Ram Manohar Lohia launched the civil-liberties movement at Margao on 18 June 1946
  • Azad Gomantak Dal: armed group (Vishwanath Lawande, Mohan Ranade and others)
  • Goa Vimochan Sahayak Samiti and satyagrahas of 1954–55
  • Operation Vijay, 19 Dec 1961; Portuguese surrender signed by Governor-General Manuel António Vassalo e Silva
  • First Lt Governor (military): K. P. Candeth; first Governor of the state (1987): Gopal Singh
  • First CM: Dayanand Bandodkar (1963); first woman CM: Shashikala Kakodkar (1973)
  • Konkani in the 8th Schedule: 71st Amendment, 1992; official language of Goa under the 1987 Act
  • Daman and Diu separated from Goa in 1987 (GPSC's 2024 key said 1985; 1987 is correct)

Geography

  • Area about 3,702 sq km; coastline about 105 km; borders Maharashtra (north) and Karnataka (east, south)
  • Rivers: Mandovi (Mhadei) has the largest basin and enters Goa through Sattari; Zuari; Terekhol; Chapora; Sal; Talpona; Galgibag
  • Mhadei/Mahadayi water dispute: Goa vs Karnataka (and Maharashtra)
  • Dudhsagar falls on the Mandovi, on the Goa–Karnataka border
  • Highest peak: Sonsogod (about 1,167 m)
  • Islands: Chorao, Divar, St Estevam (Juvem), Cumbarjua
  • Talukas 12: North (Pernem, Bardez, Tiswadi, Bicholim, Sattari, Ponda); South (Mormugao, Salcete) and Kushavati (Quepem, Sanguem, Dharbandora, Canacona)
  • Netravali bubbling lake: Sanguem taluka

Wildlife areas

  • Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary and Mollem National Park (largest), Dharbandora/Sanguem
  • Cotigao (Canacona), Netravali (Sanguem), Mhadei (Sattari), Bondla (Ponda)
  • Dr Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary: Chorao island (mangroves)
  • State animal: gaur; state bird: flame-throated bulbul; state tree: matti (Terminalia)

Culture and monuments

  • Basilica of Bom Jesus (1605): relics of St Francis Xavier; Se Cathedral; Mae de Deus church, Saligao
  • Tambdi Surla Mahadev temple: Kadamba-era, oldest surviving temple
  • Safa Masjid, Ponda: built 1560 by Ibrahim Adil Shah
  • Mangueshi (largest, most visited temple); Shantadurga (1738); Mahalasa
  • Forts: Aguada, Reis Magos, Chapora, Tiracol (north); Cabo de Rama (south)
  • Festivals: Shigmo, Carnival, Sao Joao, Bonderam (Divar), zatras; dances: Fugdi, Dhalo, Dekhni, Ghodemodni, Mando
  • First Konkani film: Mogacho Anvddo (1950); Chicken cafreal comes from Mozambique
  • Taj Fort Aguada, Sinquerim: Goa's first five-star hotel

Goa laws and institutions

  • Comunidade: collective village landholding institution
